Names: Super Asteroids, Asteroids Ultimate, Asteroids Ultra Deluxe

Make similar to first game on VCTR-SCTR included with Atari 50th Anniversary.
Ship moves like Asteroids Deluxe, with shield and shoots with turret. Two Stick/Keyboard mouse.
First New ship, enters from any side of screen, shoots homing missiles that player can shoot, until shot down or goes off edge. Ship varies vector in one axis only. They avoid enemy by moving to opposite side of screen as player. If player in middle, stay on same side, but move towards edge.
Second New ship, enters from any side of screen goes in strait line, lays mines that player must shot, until shot down or goes off other side.
The new ships can hit rocks, and the missiles and mines can hit rocks.
